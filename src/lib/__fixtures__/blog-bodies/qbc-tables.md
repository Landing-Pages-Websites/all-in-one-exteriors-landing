<p>People are keeping their cars for a longer time. The average age of a vehicle in the US rose from 12.1 years in 2021 to 12.6 years in 2024. Experts think this will hit 15.4 years by 2030. Older cars need more repair parts and more upkeep. This trend keeps the demand high for aftermarket parts. According to the <a href="https://corporatefinance.kpmg.com/us/en/insights/2025/automotive-aftermarket-industry-update-q2-2025.html">KPMG 2025 report</a>, this aging fleet is a big driver for market growth. Distributors must handle more parts for more car models than ever before.</p>
